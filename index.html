03 / Automation

Automation & Integrations

Connect your tools. Automate repetitive work. Build better workflows.

Most businesses run on a handful of tools that don't talk to each other: a website form, a CRM, email, a spreadsheet, a calendar, a payment platform. Someone ends up copying data between them by hand.

I help businesses automate those repetitive steps and connect their platforms, so leads, contacts, and orders move where they need to go on their own. I also troubleshoot automations that have quietly stopped working.

Because I come from development, I'm comfortable when a workflow needs a custom API call, a webhook endpoint, or data that has to be cleaned up before the next step can use it.

Typical requests

  • Send website form leads into a CRM with the right tags
  • Start follow-up emails or SMS when a contact enters a pipeline stage
  • Notify the team in email or chat when something needs attention
  • Sync records between two platforms that have no built-in integration
  • Fix a Zap, scenario, or workflow that is failing or skipping data
