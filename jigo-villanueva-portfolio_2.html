01 / About

Ten years of turning designs into sites that stay online.

Based in Bulacan, Philippines, I've spent the last decade converting Photoshop and XD files into responsive WordPress builds — the kind that survive traffic spikes, plugin updates, and the client's next redesign.

Most of my work comes from long-term relationships with web agencies in the US, Australia, and Singapore, which means I'm used to picking up a client's existing codebase, house style, and workflow quickly — not just building from a blank canvas.

That work now extends past the build itself: setting up Go High Level, ClickFunnels, and AI-assisted automation so a site doesn't just look right — it captures leads, follows up, and keeps working long after the visitor is gone.
